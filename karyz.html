Frequently asked questions

What is Karyz?

Karyz is an online store builder. You add your products, connect payments and share your store link, with no coding needed.

How long does it take to create a store?

Most sellers go live the same day. Sign up, add products, connect a payment gateway and you’re ready for orders.

Which payment methods can I accept?

You can connect secure gateways such as Stripe, PayPal and Razorpay, and also offer cash on delivery.

Can I use my own brand and domain?

Yes. Every plan supports your logo and colours. Business adds a custom domain, and Enterprise offers full white-labelling.

Will my store work on phones?

Yes. Every store is responsive and checkout is tuned for mobile screens.
